**Audience:** company users (businesses that post jobs and hire subcontractors).
**Covers:** the Traids web app, as seen by a company. Screen names, button labels and messages are
quoted exactly as they appear in the app.
**Last updated:** 22 September 2026

## 1. Quick facts

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Currency | British pounds (£) |
| Platform fee | **5%** of each worker's gross pay, added to your invoice. Subcontractors don't pay it |
| Card processing fee | Added to each invoice: **UK card 1.5% + 20p**, **EEA card 2.5% + 20p**, **other cards 3.25% + 20p** |
| When an invoice is created | Automatically, once **every** timesheet on a job for a given week is approved |
| Invoices cover | One job, one week, all workers on it |
| Invoice due date | **30 days** after it's created |
| Timesheet weeks | **Monday to Sunday**. Week 1 is the week the job starts |
| Automatic submission | At the end of each week, any week a worker has logged hours for but not submitted is submitted for them. You'll see these on **Monday morning** |
| Review window | **18 hours** from submission. A timesheet you haven't approved by then is **approved automatically** |
| CIS | Deducted at **30%** of each worker's gross pay. You're responsible for paying it to HMRC |
| Unpaid invoices | Reminders are sent, then features are restricted until the invoice is paid |
| Editing a job | Only while it's **Pending** (not started) |
| Staying logged in | Up to **30 days**, or until you click **Logout** |

## 2. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Job** | Work you post on the job board. Subcontractors **apply** to it |
| **Offer** | A job you send **directly** to one subcontractor. They accept or reject it |
| **Applicant / application** | A subcontractor who applied for your job, with their proposed rate and message |
| **Assigned worker** | A subcontractor you've accepted (or who accepted your offer) |
| **Saved job** | A reusable job template you created with **"Save for Later"** |
| **Timesheet** | One worker's hours for one week on one job |
| **Invoice** | The bill for one job for one week, covering every worker's approved hours |
| **CIS** | The UK Construction Industry Scheme — tax deducted from subcontractor pay |
| **Compliance folder** | A per-project store for safety documents (RAMS, permits, etc.) |

**"Pending" means different things on different screens:**

| Where | "Pending" means |
|---|---|
| A **job** (My Jobs) | Posted but not started yet — still accepting applicants |
| An **application** | You haven't accepted or rejected it yet |
| A **timesheet** (Timesheets → Pending tab) | Submitted by the worker, waiting for your approval |
| An **invoice** (Financial) | Not paid yet |

## 3. What Traids is

Traids is a construction platform run by **Traids Software Ltd (company number 17253498)**. It
connects **companies**, who post construction jobs and hire workers, with **subcontractors** —
skilled tradespeople and freelancers.

As a company you can:

- **Post jobs** that subcontractors apply to
- **Find subcontractors** and send them **direct offers**
- Review **applicants** and accept or reject them
- **Chat** with subcontractors
- Review and **approve timesheets**
- Pay **invoices**, which are generated automatically from approved hours
- Keep **compliance documents** for each project (RAMS, permits, reports, incidents, drawings)
- See **reports** on spend and contractor performance

## 4. Getting started

### 4.1 How do I create a company account?

1. Go to the Traids home page and click **"Join as Company"** or **"Signup"**.
2. On **"Choose Your Account Type"**, pick **"Register as Company"**.
3. Complete the 4-step registration form. A progress bar shows "Step X of 4".

> **Important:** registration isn't saved as a draft. If you press the browser's Back button after
> step 1, you'll see **"Leave Registration?"** — choosing **"Leave"** loses everything you entered.
> Choose **"Stay"** to continue.

#### Step 1 — Business Details

All fields are required.

| Field | Example | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Company Name | Acme Construction Ltd | |
| Registration Number | 12345678 | Up to **25 characters**. Must not already be registered. **Can't be changed later** |
| VAT Number | GB 123 4567 89 | Up to **15 characters** |
| Industry Type | Construction, Recruitment, Facilities management | |

If the registration number is already used you'll see *"Registration number is already in use."*

#### Step 2 — Add Card

Enter your payment card in the **Card Details** box (card number, expiry, CVC).

- Your card is handled securely by **Stripe** — *"We never store your card number."*
- The card isn't charged here. It's saved to your account when you finish registration and is used
  later to pay invoices.
- The country your card was issued in sets the card processing fee on your invoices (section 13.3).

If the card is incomplete: *"Please enter your card details to continue."*

#### Step 3 — Contact Information

All fields are required.

| Field | Example | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Contact Name | John Smith | |
| Work Email | john.smith@example.com | Must not already be registered. **This is your login email and can't be changed later** |
| Phone Number | +44123456789 | Up to **13 characters**. Must not already be registered |
| Head Office Address | 123 Construction Way, London, UK | |
| Password | | Use the Show/Hide toggle to check it |

Messages: *"Email already in use."* / *"Phone number already in use."*

#### Step 4 — Verification

Upload documents to verify your business:

- **Company Document**
- **Insurance Certificate**
- **Health & Safety Policy** (optional)

**Only images are accepted at registration: JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 10MB each.** Only the first
file in each box is used. If your documents are PDFs, take a clear photo or screenshot — you can add
PDFs later in **Settings → Documents**.

Click **"Submit Registration"**.

#### After you submit

Your account is created, **you're signed in automatically**, and your card is saved. You'll see
**"You're all set!"** — click **"Go to Dashboard"**.

> If the card step fails after your account was created, your account still exists. Contact Traids
> support to add your card.

### 4.2 How do I log in?

1. Click **"Login"** on the home page.
2. On **"Choose Your Account Type"**, pick **"Login as Company"**.
3. Enter your **work email** and **password** and click **"Login"**.

> **"I can't log in with the right password."** Make sure you chose **Login as Company**, not Login as
> Subcontractor. The account type is chosen on that first screen — the login form has no switch.

There's no "Remember me" option and no Google sign-in.

### 4.3 How long do I stay logged in?

You stay logged in — even after closing the browser — for up to **30 days**, or until you click
**Logout**. When your session ends you'll be sent back to the account type screen and will need to log
in again. Logging out in one tab logs you out of your other open tabs within a few seconds.

> On a shared or public computer, always click **Logout** when you're finished.

### 4.4 How do I log out?

Click your **company logo/initial** in the top-right corner and choose **"Logout"**.

### 4.5 I forgot my password

1. On the login screen click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Enter your email and click **"Reset Password"**. (If asked, choose your account type: company.)
3. We email you a **6-digit code**. Enter it on the "Two-Step Verification" screen. The code expires
   after **10 minutes**.
   - Didn't get it? Check spam, then click **"Resend it"**.
4. Choose a new password — **at least 8 characters**, and both boxes must match.
5. You'll see **"Password Reset Successfully!"** Click **"Continue"** and log in.

### 4.6 How do I change my password while logged in?

There's currently no password change option in company Settings. Log out and use **"Forgot
Password?"** on the login screen to set a new one.

### 4.7 Do I need to connect HMRC?

No. Connecting an HMRC account isn't required to post jobs, approve timesheets or pay invoices.

## 7. Posting a job

### 7.1 How do I post a job?

1. Click **"Post New Job"** on the Dashboard.
2. Fill in the form:

| Field | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Job Title** | Yes | |
| **Trade Required** | Yes | Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Masonry |
| **Description** | Yes | Supports **bold**, *italic* and bullet lists |
| **Site Address** | Yes | Full address |
| **Timeline** | Yes | Start and end date. Can't be in the past; end can't be before start |
| **Hourly Rate** | Yes | In £ per hour |
| **Workers Needed** | No | How many subcontractors you need (default 1) |
| **Documents** | No | Site maps, drawings etc. **Images only** (JPG, PNG, WEBP), up to 10MB each, up to 3 files |

3. Check the **Job Preview** and **Financial Summary** on the right.
4. Click **"Publish Job"**.

You'll see **"Job Posted Successfully"** — your job is now visible to subcontractors on their Job
Board. It starts with the status **Pending**.

### 7.2 What does the job cost me?

The **Financial Summary** shows the hourly cost including Traids' **5% platform fee**:

- Estimated Hourly Cost: £X
- Platform Fee (5%): +£Y
- **Total Per Hour: £X × 1.05**

*Example:* a £30/hour rate costs **£31.50/hour** including the fee.

This is the hourly cost only. Your actual invoice also deducts **CIS** and adds a **card processing
fee** — see section 13.3 for a full worked example. You only pay for hours you approve.

### 7.3 How do I save a job to use later? (Saved Jobs)

Click **"Save for Later"** at any point on the Post New Job form — no fields are required. You'll see
*"Draft saved. Come back anytime to finish it."*

Saved jobs are in **My Jobs → Saved**. Click **"Publish Job"** on one to open it in the form
pre-filled, finish or change it, then publish.

A saved job is a **reusable template**: publishing creates a new live job and the saved job **stays**
in the Saved tab, so you can post similar jobs again without retyping them.

If a saved job is missing required details when you publish, the app tells you which fields to fill
in (title, trade, description, site address, both timeline dates and hourly rate).

### 7.4 What is "Generate With AI"?

The **"Generate With AI"** button on the Post Job screen lets you describe the job in a sentence (for
example "need an electrician to rewire an office"). It fills in a suggested **trade**, **title** and a
**description template** (Job Overview, Responsibilities, Requirements) for you to edit.

Always review and edit the suggested text before publishing — it's a starting template.

### 7.5 How do I edit or delete a job?

You can edit or delete a job **only while it's Pending** (before it starts).

- **Edit:** click the **pencil** icon on the job (My Jobs or Dashboard). Change the details and click
  **"Update Job"**.
- **Delete:** click the **bin** icon and confirm **"Delete Job"**. *This can't be undone.*

Once a job is **In Progress** or **Completed**, it can't be edited or deleted.

## 8. My Jobs

**My Jobs** ("Manage your active listings and ongoing projects") lists everything you've posted.

### 8.1 Jobs vs Offers

Use the toggle at the top right:

- **Jobs** — jobs you posted on the job board, which subcontractors apply to
- **Offers** — jobs you created by sending an offer directly to a specific subcontractor

### 8.2 Tabs

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every live job (saved jobs aren't included) |
| **In Progress** | Jobs where work has started |
| **Pending** | Posted, not started yet — accepting applicants |
| **Completed** | Finished jobs |
| **Saved** | Saved job templates (section 7.3) |

Use **"Search jobs..."** or **Filter** by trade.

### 8.3 Job statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Posted but not started. Subcontractors can apply, and you can edit or delete it |
| **In Progress** | Work has started. Workers log hours in Timesheets |
| **Completed** | The job is finished |

### 8.4 How do I start or complete a job?

- On a **Pending** job, click **"Start Job"**. It moves to **In Progress**, and assigned workers can
  start logging hours. The job's timesheet weeks count from the week you start it.
- On an **In Progress** job, click **"Complete Job"**. It moves to **Completed**.

You can do this from the job card in My Jobs or from the job's details page.

> Once a job is **Completed**, workers can no longer submit timesheets for it. Make sure all hours are
> submitted and approved before completing it.

### 8.5 Can I cancel a job that has already started?

No. An **In Progress** job can't be cancelled or deleted — it can only be marked **Completed**. If
you need to stop work early, agree it with the workers in **Chats**, make sure their hours are
approved, then click **"Complete Job"**.

### 8.6 How do I share a job with a subcontractor?

Click the **share** icon on a job card, choose a subcontractor from **"Select Subcontractor"**, and
click **"Share"**. The job is sent to them in a chat. You'll see **"Job Shared!"**

### 8.7 What happens when a job has enough workers?

When the number of accepted workers reaches **Workers Needed**, the job is full:

- It disappears from the subcontractors' **Job Board**
- New applications and offers for it are blocked
- Any applications still pending are **rejected automatically**

To take on more workers, you'd need a job with a higher **Workers Needed** — it can only be changed
while the job is still **Pending** (section 7.5).

## 9. Reviewing applicants

### 9.1 How do I see who applied for my job?

In **My Jobs**, click the job card. The details page shows the **Job Description** and, below it,
**Applicants & Requests**.

For a Pending job this lists everyone who **applied**, plus anyone you **sent an offer** to. Each card
shows the subcontractor's photo, name, trade, rating, rate, years of experience, city and their
**message** (cover letter). The rate shown is the **hourly rate they proposed**, which can differ from
your job's rate.

You'll also get a **"New Applicant!"** pop-up whenever someone applies.

If nobody has applied: **"No Applicants Yet"** — *"Once subcontractors apply or receive offers for
this job, they will appear here."*

### 9.2 How do I accept or reject an applicant?

Click **"Accept"** or **"Reject"** on their card. The card then shows an **Accepted** (green) or
**Rejected** (red) badge, and **the decision can't be changed**.

Once the job starts, accepted workers appear as the job's **assigned workers**.

> **Good to know:**
> - A rejected subcontractor **can't apply to that job again**.
> - When a job is full, remaining pending applications are rejected automatically (section 8.7).
> - Opening a job from a **notification** can open a summary page where Accept/Reject don't work.
>   To manage applicants, always open the job from **My Jobs**.

### 9.3 Which rate is the worker paid — mine or theirs?

When you accept an applicant, they're paid the **hourly rate they proposed** on their application.
Check the rate on their applicant card before accepting.

## 10. Finding subcontractors and sending offers

### 10.1 How do I find a subcontractor?

Go to **Find Subcontractors** ("Discover and book top-rated professionals for your projects").

Filters on the left:

- **Search** — "Search name or skill..."
- **Trade Type** — Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, HVAC Tech, Painter, Masonry
- **Max Hourly Rate** — slider
- **Location**
- **Years Of Experience** — minimum years

Each result shows the subcontractor's name, trade, rating, a short "About" and their **hourly rate**.
Click **"View Profile"**.

No results: **"No Subcontractor Found"** — try fewer filters.

### 10.2 What's on a subcontractor's profile?

- Name, trade, location and **Availability** (Available / Unavailable)
- **About**
- **Compliance Center** — their **Public Liability Insurance**, **Site Tickets** and **Trade
  Certifications**, each with an expiry date. **"Expiring soon...."** is shown in red when a document
  expires within 90 days, and **"Not provided"** if it's missing. Click **"Download Copy"** to view a
  document.
- **Portfolio** — past projects the subcontractor has published, with photos and project details.
  These are added by the subcontractor and aren't verified by Traids.
- **Reviews** — ratings and comments from companies they've worked with
- Buttons: **"Message"** and **"Book Now"**

> Check a subcontractor's compliance documents before you book them.

### 10.3 How do I message a subcontractor?

On their profile click **"Message"**. Write your message, optionally **Attach Files** (images, PDF,
Word or Excel, up to 10MB each), and click **"Send Message"**. You'll see **"Message Sent!"** and the
conversation continues in **Chats**.

### 10.4 How do I send a job offer directly to a subcontractor?

1. On their profile click **"Book Now"**.
2. On **"Sending Offer"**, either:
   - pick one of your **Pending** jobs from **"SELECT EXISTING JOB"**, or
   - leave it on **"Start from scratch..."** and fill in the job details (title, trade, description,
     site address, timeline, hourly rate, workers needed, documents — PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10MB, up
     to 3 files).
3. Click **"Send Offer"**.

You'll see **"Offer Sent Successfully"** — *"Wait for the subcontractor to accept."* The offer
appears in **My Jobs → Offers**. You'll get a notification when the subcontractor accepts or rejects
it.

> If you pick an existing job, the job is sent **as it is** — edits made in the offer form aren't
> saved. To change the job, edit it first from My Jobs, then send the offer.

### 10.5 Can I cancel an offer after sending it?

There's no option to withdraw an offer once it's sent. You can:

- **Ask the subcontractor to reject it** — message them in **Chats**. This is the safest option.
- **Delete the job**, if it's still **Pending**, with the **bin** icon in **My Jobs**. This removes
  the offer from the subcontractor's Offers tab — but it deletes the **whole job**, including
  **every application** and its **compliance folder**. If you sent one of your job board posts as the
  offer, everyone who applied to it loses their application too. *This can't be undone.*

### 10.6 What is the Bio Analyser?

**"Bio Analyser"** on the Find Subcontractors page lets you describe what you need in a sentence
(e.g. "experienced plumber in Manchester"). It shows up to 6 matching subcontractors with a **fit
score** and a tier — **Top Match**, **Strong**, **Good** or **Fair** — based on their rating,
experience, skills, availability and location. From each card you can **View Profile** or **Send
Offer**.

Use it as a quick shortlist, then check each profile yourself before booking.

## 12. Timesheets and approval

### 12.1 How do timesheets work?

Once a job is **In Progress**, each worker logs their daily hours (check-in and check-out times) and
**submits** the week to you. You review and **approve** it. When every worker's timesheet for that
week is approved, an **invoice** is created.

Weeks run **Monday to Sunday**, starting from the week the job was started. Workers can only log and
submit the current week.

### 12.2 When do timesheets arrive?

Workers can submit any time during the week. If a worker has logged hours but hasn't submitted by
the end of the week, the week is **submitted automatically** for them — so expect a batch of
timesheets waiting for you on **Monday morning**.

### 12.3 How do I review a timesheet?

1. Go to **Timesheets** ("Review work logs and generate professional project invoices").
2. Click a job card (it shows the number of **Workers** and the **Rate**).
3. The **Pending** tab lists timesheets waiting for you. The table shows each **Worker**, the
   **review countdown** (time left to review), **Hours**, **Payable** amount, submission **Date** and
   **Status**.
4. Click the **eye** icon to see the day-by-day breakdown — each day's date, hours, **Checked In**
   and **Checked Out** times.

### 12.4 How do I approve a timesheet?

- **One at a time:** click the **✓ (check)** icon on the row. It moves to the **Approved** tab.
- **All at once:** click **"Approve All"**, then **"Yes, Approve All"**. This approves every pending
  timesheet on that job, across all weeks. *This can't be undone.*

When all timesheets for a week are approved, the invoice is **generated automatically** — you'll see
*"N invoice(s) generated"*.

### 12.5 How long do I have to review, and what if I don't?

You have **18 hours** from submission to review each timesheet. The review column counts down the time
left (e.g. "5h 12m left"). Approved rows show "Review Closed".

**If you haven't approved a timesheet within 18 hours, it's approved automatically** and counts
towards the invoice exactly as if you'd approved it. So if hours look wrong, raise it with the worker
straight away.

### 12.6 Timesheet statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Submitted by the worker, waiting for your review |
| **Approved** | Approved by you or automatically after 18 hours; included in an invoice |

### 12.7 Can I reject a timesheet or ask for changes?

There's no reject button in the app, and a submitted timesheet can't be sent back for changes. If
hours look wrong, message the worker in **Chats** as soon as possible — before the timesheet is
approved automatically — and contact Traids support if it can't be resolved.

### 12.8 Why hasn't an invoice been created yet?

An invoice needs **every** timesheet on that job for that week to be approved. If one worker's
timesheet is still pending, the invoice waits for it. It will be created at the latest when that
timesheet is approved automatically, 18 hours after submission.

### 12.9 How do I rate a subcontractor?

On an **approved** timesheet, click the **star** icon. Choose **1–5 stars** (required), add an optional
comment, and click **"Submit & Complete"**. You can rate each subcontractor once per job — after that
the star shows "Already rated".

Ratings appear on the subcontractor's profile and help other companies.

## 13. Invoices and payment

### 13.1 Where do I see my invoices?

- **Financial → Project Invoices** lists every invoice with its number, project, amount, date and
  status (**Paid** or **Pending**). Use **"Search..."** to find one.
- **Timesheets → (job) → "Invoices"** shows invoices for one job, marked **PAID** or **UNPAID**.

Click the **eye** icon (or **"Pay Invoice"** / **"View Receipt"**) to open an invoice.

### 13.2 What's on an invoice?

- Invoice number, project, site address, date, **due date** (30 days after the invoice is created) and
  status
- One line per worker: **Hours**, **Rate**, **Gross Amount**, **CIS Rate**, **CIS Deduction** and
  **Payable Amount**
- **Subtotal**
- **Platform Fee (5%)**
- **Total CIS Deduction** (if any)
- **Stripe Processing Fee** (if any)
- **Total Due**

### 13.3 How is the invoice total worked out?

For each worker:

- **Gross amount** = hours × hourly rate
- **CIS deduction** = gross × 30%
- **Platform fee** = gross × 5%
- **Payable** = gross − CIS deduction + platform fee

Then:

- **Card processing fee** = (total of all workers' payable amounts) × card rate + 20p. Card rate:
  **1.5%** for a UK card, **2.5%** for an EEA card, **3.25%** for any other card.
- **Total Due** = total payable + card processing fee

**Worked example** — one worker, 40 hours at £25/hour, CIS at 30%, paid with a UK card:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross (40 × £25) | £1,000.00 |
| CIS deduction (30%) | −£300.00 |
| Platform fee (5% of gross) | +£50.00 |
| Payable | £750.00 |
| Card processing fee (£750 × 1.5% + 20p) | +£11.45 |
| **Total Due** | **£761.45** |

The worker receives **£700** (gross minus CIS). The £50 platform fee goes to Traids.

### 13.4 How do I pay an invoice?

1. Open an unpaid invoice. Your saved card is shown (last 4 digits, expiry, brand).
2. Click **"Pay Now"**.
3. The payment is processed: *Payment intent created → Charging card → Funds received → Distributing
   to subcontractors*.
4. You'll see **"£X Paid"** — *"Your invoice has been paid and subcontractors have been notified.
   Funds will reach their accounts within 1–2 business days."* It also lists each subcontractor's
   payout.

If payment fails you'll see the reason, or *"Payment failed. Please try again."* Check your card with
your bank.

### 13.5 What happens after I pay?

Traids pays each worker their share (gross minus CIS) automatically once your payment is confirmed —
you don't need to do anything else. The invoice changes to **Paid**.

### 13.6 When do I have to pay?

Each invoice is due **30 days** after it's created. Paying sooner gets your workers paid sooner.

If an invoice isn't paid by its due date, Traids will send you reminders. If it stays unpaid, many
features of your account will be **restricted until the invoice is paid**.

### 13.7 Which card is charged? Can I change it?

The card you added during registration. There's currently no screen in the app to view, add or change
your card — contact Traids support if you need to update it.

### 13.8 How do I download an invoice?

In **Financial → Project Invoices**, click the **download** icon. The invoice downloads as a file you
can open in your browser and print or save as PDF from there.

### 13.9 What is CIS, and how does it affect me?

The **Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)** is a UK tax scheme where tax is deducted from payments to
subcontractors. On Traids:

- CIS is deducted at **30%** of each worker's gross pay. It's shown on each line of the invoice.
- The CIS amount is **deducted** from what you pay through Traids — it isn't collected by Traids or
  paid to the worker.
- **You're responsible** for paying the deducted CIS to HMRC and filing your CIS returns. Traids
  doesn't do this for you.

For what you owe HMRC or how to file, speak to your accountant or HMRC — Traids can't give tax advice.

## 14. Compliance

### 14.1 What is the Compliance Center?

**Compliance** ("Manage safety documents, permits, and regulatory requirements according to Jobs")
gives you a **folder for each project**, where you store its safety and regulatory documents. A folder
is created automatically for each job you post — if you have no jobs yet you'll see **"No Projects
Yet"** with a **"Post a Job"** button.

### 14.2 How do I upload a compliance document?

## 19. The job lifecycle, start to finish

1. **Post a job** (it's **Pending**) — or **send an offer** directly to a subcontractor.
2. **Review applicants** in the job's **Applicants & Requests** and **Accept** the ones you want.
3. Click **"Start Job"** — the job is **In Progress**.
4. Workers **log hours** and **submit** each week (automatically at the end of the week if they
   forget).
5. **Review and approve** timesheets within 18 hours. Anything you don't approve by then is approved
   automatically.
6. When every timesheet for a week is approved, an **invoice** is generated automatically. **Pay** it
   with **"Pay Now"** within 30 days. Workers are paid automatically.
7. **Rate** each subcontractor.
8. Click **"Complete Job"** — the job is **Completed**.

## 20. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Answer |

| Payment failed | Check the card with your bank; contact Traids support to change the card |

## 21. Known app issues and accuracy notes

Some screens show information that's misleading or controls that don't work yet. Use the correct
information below when answering.

- **Review window label.** If the Timesheets table still shows a **"12H Review"** countdown or
  "Review Overdue" after 12 hours, that's outdated — the review window is **18 hours**, and a
  timesheet is approved automatically when it ends.
- **No subscriptions.** There's no "Subscription" section in Financial. Companies pay the 5% platform
  fee per invoice. Don't describe subscription plans.
- **Financial tabs.** Invoices are under **"Project Invoices"** (the only tab).
- **Invoice download** is an HTML file, not a PDF. Tell users to open it and print or save as PDF.
- **"Funds will reach their accounts within 1–2 business days"** on the payment screen refers to the
  subcontractors being paid, not to anything the company needs to do.
- **Old job-details page.** Opening a job or offer from a notification goes to a page where
  "Complete Job", "Accept", "Reject" and "Message" don't work. Always send users to **My Jobs → job
  card**.
- **Rate labels.** Edit Job and Send Offer show "/day" and sometimes "$" in the cost note. The rate is
  **hourly** and in **£**.
- **Trade spelling and lists.** Post Job shows "Masanory" (means Masonry). Trade lists differ by
  screen: Post/Edit Job has 4 trades; Send Offer adds HVAC, Painter, Roofing, Landscaping; the Find
  filter has 6.
- **Document rules differ.** Registration accepts images only; Settings accepts PDF/JPG/PNG. Tell
  users with PDFs to add them in Settings after registering.
- **Registration asterisks.** Company Document and Insurance Certificate are marked required but the
  app doesn't block submitting without them.
- **Send Offer with an existing job** sends the saved job; edits made in the offer form are
  discarded.
- **Chat errors.** If sending a message fails, no error is shown. If a user says their message didn't
  arrive, suggest refreshing and resending.
- **"Generate With AI" and "Bio Analyser"** are rule-based helpers, not a live AI model. Describe
  them as suggestions to review, and don't claim deep analysis.
- **Placeholders and controls that don't work yet.** "Job Success: N/A" on subcontractor profiles;
  the "Min Rating" filter on
  Find Subcontractors; the Timesheets "Filter", Hours / Hourly Rate / Fee Percentage dropdowns;
  Financial Previous/Next; "Edit Details" on compliance; "Create Folder"; "Invite to Job" (shows
  "coming soon"); the Dashboard "This Week" label. Don't suggest users rely on these.
- **Compliance "Expiring soon".** The company's view of a subcontractor profile flags documents
  expiring within **90 days**; the subcontractor's own Profile Overview uses **30 days**.
